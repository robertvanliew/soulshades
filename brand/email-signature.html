How to install in Gmail
  1. Click inside the white box below, press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
  2. Gmail → Settings → See all settings → General → Signature → edit "Soul Shades Music".
  3. Delete the old signature, paste with Ctrl+V, scroll down and click Save Changes.
